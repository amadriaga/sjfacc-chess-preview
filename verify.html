Commemorative Founders’ Coin

Verify a coin

Scan the QR code on the Certificate of Authenticity to check a coin.

How to check a coin you are buying

  1. Scan the QR code on its Certificate of Authenticity. This page must say Genuine.
  2. The number shown here must match the number engraved on the coin (for example No. FC27-02) and the number on the certificate’s hologram sticker.
  3. The registered owner shown here should be the person selling it, or they should have a record of buying it.

Founders’ Coins were issued only once, at SJFACC’s Inaugural Club Night (January 11, 2027), and are never made again. Questions? Contact us.
