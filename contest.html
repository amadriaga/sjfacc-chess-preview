How it works

  1. 1
    EnterAnyone can send up to 3 designs. Under-18s need a parent or guardian’s OK.
  2. 2
    CheckedA club officer checks each entry before it appears in the gallery.
  3. 3
    VoteRegistered members tick their favourite and confirm with a code emailed to them. One vote per member.
  4. 4
    WinnerThe design with the most votes becomes the 2028 season shirt, printed with its designer’s credit, e.g. “Designed by Juan dela Cruz”, and its designer wins a free one-year membership and a free 2028 season T-shirt. Results are announced when voting closes.
  5. 5
    OwnershipThe winning design becomes the property of SJFACC. Its designer may not use it in any way, for example to sell shirts not endorsed by SJFACC, without SJFACC’s prior written approval. Designs that don’t win stay with their designers; the club only shows them in the gallery.
  6. 6
    ForeverThe winning design joins our Shirt Editions gallery forever: one edition per season, like a collector’s card.

Dates, the full rules and who can win will be announced by the club. Questions? Contact us.
