Website info

How this website works

What this site is built with, how it keeps you safe, and what it does with your information. This is Build 315, published November 26, 2026.

The basics

SJFACC website is a set of plain web pages, running on Visum Systems, LLC’s sandbox servers and protected by Cloudflare. There is no database, no user accounts and no server program behind it, which leaves very little that can be attacked or go wrong. Every change is saved with a new build number, shown at the bottom of every page, so we always know which version you are looking at.

Security

CloudflareThe site is reached through Cloudflare, which protects it from attacks and floods of fake traffic.
Encrypted connection (HTTPS)Every page is sent over an encrypted connection (TLS 1.3 with 256-bit AES encryption); plain, unencrypted access is switched to HTTPS automatically. Older, weaker encryption (below TLS 1.2) is not accepted.
Content Security PolicyYour browser is told the page may only load its own files, and may only send forms to the two services named below (Google and Formspree). Anything else is blocked, even if someone tried to sneak it in.
No framingOther websites can’t show our pages inside theirs (a common trick to fool visitors).
No camera, microphone or locationThe site never asks for them, and the browser is told to refuse if anything tried.
Your sign-up formSent with TLS 1.3 and 256-bit AES encryption (never weaker than TLS 1.2) straight to the club’s Google account, which has 2-step verification; Google stores it encrypted. Only club officers can see forms, after signing in with a one-time Google Authenticator code. See “Your information is protected” on the Register page.
Spam protectionEach form has a hidden spam trap that catches automated junk.

These protections apply on staging.sjfacc.org.

Your privacy

No trackingNo cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no outside scripts or fonts.
Sign-ups and advertising enquiriesSaved in a Google Sheet that only club officers can open. No email copies are sent.
Sensitive informationDetails about players under 18, the optional allergies or medical answer, emergency contacts and signed forms are kept only in the club’s own Google account (2-step verification, stored encrypted) and seen only by club officers. No card or bank details, dates of birth or home addresses are collected. See Privacy: Sensitive information.
Contact messagesDelivered to the club by Formspree, a form service.
Payments and donationsTo be announced. Card and bank details will never pass through this website.

Full details: Privacy & Disclaimer.

Software and tools

Web pagesHTML, CSS and JavaScript, written with the help of AI-assisted tools, with no frameworks or add-ons.
HostingServed by GitHub Pages on a Visum Systems, LLC sandbox sub-domain (o2bot.com), protected by Cloudflare.
FormsGoogle Sheets with Google Apps Script (sign-ups, advertising); Formspree (contact).
PaymentsTo be announced.
QR codesQR codes on the pages, flyers, forms and presentations made with segno; the sign-in set-up code for officers is drawn in your browser with QR Code Generator for JavaScript by Kazuhiko Arase (MIT licence). “QR Code” is a registered trademark of DENSO WAVE INCORPORATED.
PrintablesPrintable PDFs (flyers, program, forms, agendas, minutes) made with the Chromium browser through Playwright.
Logo and imagesThe crest’s sizes and versions (website icons, the round crest, cut-outs and web images) made with Pillow (the Python Imaging Library) and SciPy image tools.
PresentationsBoard presentations (such as the 5-minute overview) made with PptxGenJS and turned into PDF with LibreOffice; their QR codes checked with ZXing.
BuildingBuilt by Visum Systems, LLC. All files are kept in version control (Git), with an off-site backup on GitLab.

Who builds this website

DeveloperThe website is designed, built and looked after by Visum Systems, LLC. Its development was greatly enhanced and simplified with AI-assisted tools.
Official websitechess.fassj.o2bot.com is SJFACC’s current official website. It is fully operational and works as the club’s interim live (production) site.
ServersThe site runs on Visum Systems, LLC’s sandbox servers, under a sub-domain of o2bot.com. It won’t work as intended outside the o2bot.com sub-domain sandbox without a proper migration and server reconfiguration.

See a problem?

If something looks wrong or broken, please let us know and mention the build number at the bottom of the page.
