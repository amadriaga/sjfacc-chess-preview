Privacy & Disclaimer

Your information, photos and this website

How SJFACC handles the information you give us, the photos we take at club events, and the information on this site.

Interim version — under review

This page is a working version for review by the club committee, legal counsel and the SJFACC Board. It will be finalized and adopted after it is presented to the Board. Items marked Board to confirm are still to be decided.

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Effective date: set when adopted by the Board Board to confirm

1. Who we are

This website, staging.sjfacc.org, is run by SJFACC, the South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Community, Inc. (“the club”, “we”), founded on November 17, 2026. Board to confirm

2. Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us:

  • Online sign-up (Register): your name, email, optionally your phone, whether you are an adult or under 18 (and, for players under 18, a parent or guardian’s name), your T-shirt size, and optionally how many extra shirts you’d like (and their sizes), your chess experience, how you heard about us and a short note.
  • 2028 T-shirt contest (contest page): entrants give their name, email, age group (and for under-18s a parent or guardian’s name and OK) and their design; the gallery shows only the design, its title and the entrant’s first name. The winning design becomes the property of SJFACC (see the contest terms). Voters give their registered email to receive a one-time code by email; we record which design each member voted for, and never publish it.
  • Contact form (Contact): your name, email, optionally your phone, and your message.
  • Membership form and waiver (Register), signed online or on the printed form: the player’s full name and age group (and for under-18s a parent or guardian’s name), phone and email; chess experience and, optionally, a US Chess ID; T-shirt size and any extra shirts; optionally, any allergies or medical conditions (used only for the player’s safety at club activities); an emergency contact; the photo and video choice (see section 6) and how you want to receive club news; and the signature and date. We don’t ask for a date of birth or a home address. Online, you sign by typing your name after agreeing to sign electronically; we keep a PDF of the signed form with the date and time, your browser type and a code that shows whether the answers were ever changed, and a club officer approves it.

Online membership forms and advertising enquiries are stored in a Google Sheet, and the signed membership forms as PDFs in a Google Drive folder, that only club officers can open. Contact form messages are delivered to the club officers by Formspree, a form service, which keeps a copy for a limited time. Payment and donation options are to be announced; they will be handled outside this website, and we will never see your card details.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you. The companies that host and protect the site (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) handle standard technical information, such as your IP address, so they can deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

Sensitive information

Some answers on the membership form are sensitive: details about players under 18 and their parents or guardians, the optional allergies or medical conditions, emergency contacts, and your signed form. This is how we look after them:

  • Where: only in the club’s own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive). The account is protected by 2-step verification, and Google stores the information encrypted.
  • On the way: everything you send on this website travels over an encrypted connection (TLS 1.3 with 256-bit AES encryption).
  • Who can see it: only club officers, after signing in with a one-time code. The public “Who’s registered” list shows only a first name and last initial, and only if you choose to be listed.
  • What we never ask for or keep: card or bank details (payments never go through this website), dates of birth, home addresses or Social Security numbers.
  • Your choice: the medical box is optional. You can leave it blank and tell a club officer in person instead.

We take reasonable care to protect your information, but no website or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please share only what the form asks for, and contact us if you have any concerns.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to run the club: to contact you about membership, club nights, events and programs, to organise games and groups at the right level, and to keep basic club records. We do not use it for anything else.

4. Who can see it

  • Club officers who need it to run the club. Board to confirm
  • Our partner organizations, only when needed, for example our venue in an emergency.

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t share it with sponsors or other organisations without your permission, unless the law requires it.

How your information is protected. Everything you send on this website is encrypted on the way with TLS 1.3 and 256-bit AES encryption, the same standard banks use; weaker connections (older than TLS 1.2) are refused. Sign-up and contest forms go straight to the club’s own Google account, which has 2-step verification, and Google stores them encrypted. Contact messages go through Formspree to the club’s email. Only club officers can see forms, after signing in with a one-time Google Authenticator code. Details: Website info → Security.

5. Children and young players

Young players are a big part of our club. For any member under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the membership form and sign it. We only use a young player’s information to run club activities, and a parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete it. Board to confirm

6. Photos and videos

We take photos and videos at club nights, tournaments and events, and we may share them on this website and on the club’s social media pages to celebrate our members and promote the club. Board to confirm

  • You choose. The membership form asks you to tick I agree or I do not agree. If you choose I do not agree, we will do our best not to publish photos in which you, or your child, can be identified.
  • Young players: we don’t publish the full names of players under 18 alongside their photos.
  • You can change your mind. Ask us at any time and we will remove a photo or video of you or your child from our website and our own social media pages within a reasonable time.
  • Events are often held in shared or public spaces, and other attendees or venues may take their own photos. We can’t control photos we didn’t publish.

7. Tournament results and ratings

If you play in a rated event, your name, results and rating are reported to the rating organisation (such as US Chess or FIDE). Those organisations publish results publicly under their own rules, which we don’t control. Club standings and results may also be posted on this website.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep members’ information while they are members and for up to 2 years afterwards. If you register interest but don’t go on to join, we delete your information after 12 months. Board to confirm

9. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the information we hold about you or your child
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop contacting you
  • change your photo permission, or remove a photo or video

10. Information on this website

We publish the information on this website in good faith, for general information only. We work to keep it accurate and up to date, but schedules, venues and details can change, and we can’t guarantee that everything here is complete or current. Please confirm important details, such as event dates and locations, with the club before relying on them. The club is not responsible for any loss that results from relying on information on this website.

11. Links to other websites

This website may link to other sites, such as chess federations, tournament pages or venues. We don’t control those sites, and a link doesn’t mean we endorse everything on them. They have their own privacy policies and terms, so please check them before sharing any information.

12. Club events and waivers

This page covers the website and the information you give us through it. Taking part in club events, including any liability waiver, code of conduct or parent consent for young players, is covered by separate forms that the club provides when membership and events open. Board to confirm

13. Changes to this page

We may update this page as the club grows. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top, and we will tell members about any important changes.

14. Contact us

For questions about this page, or to make any of the requests in sections 6 and 9, use our contact form or message us on our Facebook page. Board to confirm
