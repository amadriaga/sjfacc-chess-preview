Your information is protected

  • Encrypted on the way: everything sent from this page uses TLS 1.3 with 256-bit AES encryption; weaker connections (older than TLS 1.2) are refused.
  • Kept in the club’s own account: forms, documents and requests go straight to the club’s Google account (2-step verification), stored encrypted by Google.
  • Officers only: nothing here is shown without signing in with a one-time code from Google Authenticator, and an access request grants nothing until an officer sends an invitation.
