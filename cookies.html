Cookies

Cookies

The SJFACC website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t sell or share information about your visit.

What the site does use

  • Cloudflare security: Cloudflare, which protects this site, may set a security cookie to tell real visitors from harmful traffic. It holds no personal details.
  • “I’m human” check: some forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to stop spam. It checks your browser without tracking you across websites.
  • Your own browser storage: the site may remember small things on your device, like a page you had open; officers’ sign-in is kept in their own browser. You can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Links to other sites (like Facebook or Google Maps) follow those sites’ own cookie rules.
