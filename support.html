Partners

TCGSCHCHERRY HILLVENUE▾

The Church of the Good Shepherd Cherry Hill (TCGSCH)

Our community club nights are held at The Church of the Good Shepherd in Cherry Hill. We are deeply grateful to Senior Pastor Darius Nable and the TCGSCH community for generously opening their doors and giving our members a warm home to play.

Visit tcgsch.org
FEAST MANTUAMANTUAMEET & PLAY▾

The Feast Mantua

The Feast Mantua is a Catholic prayer meeting in Mantua, New Jersey, and part of the Light of Jesus Family. The Feast is the weekly gathering of this Catholic community, founded by lay preacher Bo Sanchez in the Philippines, with Feasts across the Philippines and around the world. Thank you to The Feast Mantua for welcoming SJFACC. We are looking at holding chess meet-and-play sessions during one of their regular gatherings.

Visit their Facebook group
FASSJSOUTH JERSEYPARTNER▾

Filipino American Society of South Jersey (FASSJ)

Since 1974, FASSJ has brought Filipino-American families in South Jersey together through culture, scholarships and community service. SJFACC is an independent non-profit organization. Thank you to President Heidi Italia and the FASSJ officers for welcoming the club in its earliest days and for their continued friendship.

Visit the FASSJ website

Tax notes

* South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Community, Inc. (SJFACC) is a New Jersey nonprofit corporation. SJFACC is applying to the IRS for 501(c)(3) status (not yet approved). Once approved, it applies from November 24, 2026.

  • Donations: tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law (nothing is given in return).
  • Membership dues: tax-deductible only above the value of the T-shirt you receive. Monday club nights and member discounts don’t reduce it.
  • Tournament entries, guest passes, extra T-shirts, ID badges and the Founders’ Coin: payments for events or items, not tax-deductible. Item prices include New Jersey sales tax. At tournaments SJFACC sells only T-shirts and ID badges; the coin is sold once, for the Inaugural Club Night only.
  • Every receipt shows what you paid, the value of anything you received, and the amount you may deduct.

This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult your tax advisor.
