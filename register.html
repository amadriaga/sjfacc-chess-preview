Membership form and waiver: please read *

1. Participation Agreement

I voluntarily join SJFACC (South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Community, Inc.) and agree to take part in its activities, including club nights, meetings, lessons, tournaments and other club events. I understand that taking part means following the club’s rules, respecting other members and showing good sportsmanship.

2. Assumption of Risk

Chess is a low-risk activity, but I understand that club activities may still involve some risks, such as slips or falls at the venue, accidents while traveling to and from events, and loss of or damage to personal belongings. I voluntarily accept these risks.

3. Release of Liability

To the fullest extent allowed by New Jersey law, I release and hold harmless SJFACC (South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Community, Inc.) and its founders, officers and volunteers, The Church of the Good Shepherd Cherry Hill (TCGSCH), and the club’s partner organizations from any claims for injury, loss or damage arising from my participation in club activities, including claims of ordinary negligence. This release does not cover gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

4. Code of Conduct

I agree to: treat all members, instructors, organizers and guests with respect; follow the club’s rules and the instructions of club officers and volunteers; play fairly and honestly: no phones, computers or outside help during games; show good sportsmanship, win or lose; take care of chess equipment, SJFACC property and the church premises, and follow the venue’s rules; avoid disruptive, unsafe or inappropriate behavior, including harassment or bullying. I understand that the club may suspend or end the membership of anyone who seriously or repeatedly breaks this code.

5. Emergency Medical Consent

If the member needs urgent medical care and the emergency contact cannot be reached, I authorize the club’s officers or volunteers to call 911 and arrange emergency treatment. I am responsible for any resulting medical costs.

6. Photos and Videos

The club may take photos and videos at club activities and use them on its website, Facebook page and printed materials.

7. Club News and Reminders

The club may send me news and reminders by the ways I choose below.

8. Privacy

The information on this form is used only for club purposes: membership records, contacting members and emergencies. It is never sold, and it is shared only with club officers and, when needed, the club’s partner organizations.

9. Acknowledgment and Consent

I have read and understand this form. I agree to its terms and sign it freely.

Parent / Legal Guardian Consent (members under 18)

I am the parent or legal guardian of the member named above. I give permission for my child to take part in club activities. I have read and understand this form and agree to its terms on my child’s behalf, to the extent allowed by law. I am responsible for supervising my child before, during and after club activities.

Your information is protected

  • Encrypted on the way: your form is sent with TLS 1.3 and 256-bit AES encryption, the same standard banks use. Weaker connections (older than TLS 1.2) are refused.
  • Kept in the club’s own account: it goes straight to the club’s Google account, which is protected by 2-step verification, and Google stores it encrypted.
  • Seen only by club officers, who sign in with a one-time code from Google Authenticator. Links to change your registration are emailed only to the address on your form and work for 15 minutes.
  • Never sold, no advertising, no tracking. The public list shows only first name and last initial, and only if you tick the box.
