New · 2027 Season Pass

One pass. Four tournaments. One is free.

Pay your membership and all four quarterly tournaments together and one tournament is on us. Your Season Pass is a wearable ID badge on a SJFACC lanyard, with your own QR code: an officer scans it on an iPad, and you’re checked in.

  • Q1 · Founders’ CupMon, Jan 11, 2027
  • Q2 · Spring OpenMon, Apr 12, 2027*
  • Q3 · Summer OpenMon, Jul 12, 2027*
  • Q4 · Fall OpenMon, Oct 11, 2027*

Register by Mon, Dec 28, 2026 and your pass is a plastic badge with your name printed on it. Walk-ins on opening night (Jan 11) wear a loaner badge that night and get their own printed badge soon after. Guests get a paper day pass at check-in.

Price to be announced. Not a member? A guest Tournament Pass gives you a day pass at all four tournaments. *Tentative dates.

Contest · 2028 season

Design our 2028 season T‑shirt

Our 2027 inaugural shirt was designed by co-founder Andrew Suganob. For 2028, we’d love your design! The winning design becomes the official 2028 season club T‑shirt, printed with “Designed by” and the designer’s name, and its designer wins a free one-year membership and a free 2028 season T‑shirt.

Anyone can enter; registered members vote. The winning design joins our Shirt Editions gallery forever: one edition per season, like a collector’s card. Dates and full rules will be announced by the club.

What we do

A club built around the board

Three commitments shape everything we organise — from casual club nights to rated competition.

♞

Play

Regular club nights with casual, rapid and blitz games — and, as the club grows, rated play for members who want to compete.

♝

Learn

Lessons, game analysis and a junior program — structured improvement for newcomers and experienced players alike.

♚

Community

A welcoming home for Filipino-American families in South Jersey — friendships made across the board.
