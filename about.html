About us

Our story begins on November 17, 2026

SJFACC was founded to give our community a place to play, learn and grow — together, over the board.

Who we are

A home for chess in South Jersey

SJFACC is a nonprofit chess community rooted in the Filipino-American tradition of family, hospitality and friendly competition. We welcome players of every age and strength, with a special focus on youth and the Filipino-American community of South Jersey — from those learning how the pieces move to experienced tournament players.

The club began as the idea of Andrew Suganob, a lifelong chess player. Through a friend in the community, he was introduced to fellow player Alex Madriaga, and together they held the club’s first meeting on November 17, 2026.

That first meeting inspired the founders to pursue a larger vision: an independent nonprofit organization, the South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Community, Inc. (SJFACC).

Our goal is to bring the community together over the board, grow toward tournaments, and discover the next generation of talent.

Our mission

To teach and promote chess for all ages, especially youth and the Filipino-American community of South Jersey, through free and low-cost instruction, practice and tournaments open to the public, and to bring families together.

Our corporate logo and emblem

The logo of South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Community, Inc. tells who we are at a glance:

  • The Philippine sun and the Philippine and American flags: our heritage and our home.
  • A gold king between a white and a black knight: friendly rivals around one board.
  • The checkerboard and “United Through Chess”: what brings us together.
  • “Est. 2026”: the year the club was founded.

Its 3-D version is our emblem, and we hope to make it the club pin one day.

Our crest

The story behind the crest

At our founding meeting on November 17, 2026, we agreed the community needed a crest of its own. It became a true team effort: the founders traded sketches and revised versions back and forth, each one building on the last, until we arrived at the crest you see here, with the South Jersey Filipino-American Chess Community, Inc. name around the ring.

What you’ll find in it:

  • The Philippine and American flags, side by side: our heritage and our home.
  • The Philippine sun, rising behind the board, for the families and traditions we carry with us.
  • A golden king, with two knights: every player, on either side of the board, one white and one black.
  • Laurel branches, for the effort, growth and good sportsmanship we celebrate at every game.
  • “United Through Chess, Est. 2026”: the community we belong to, brought together over the board.

Community officers

Leadership, chosen by our Board of Trustees

SJFACC is run by its Board of Trustees, which elects the officers; members take part in community life but don’t vote on the board. Interim officers serve until the first board meeting confirms them, and officer terms are set in the bylaws.
